---

# Versioning Policy

The project follows Semantic Versioning.

Format:

MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH

Examples:

* 1.0.0
* 1.1.0
* 1.1.5
* 2.0.0

Definitions

Major

Breaking architectural or compatibility changes.

Minor

New capabilities added without breaking compatibility.

Patch

Bug fixes, documentation updates, and internal improvements.

# Recording Policy

Record changes only after they are:

* Reviewed
* Approved
* Implemented (or officially accepted for documentation)

Do not record temporary experiments or incomplete work.

---

# Governance

The changelog shall be updated:

* At every approved release.
* After major architectural decisions.
* Following significant documentation updates.
* After accepted security improvements.
* Before every production release.

Architecture Decision Records (ADR) should be referenced where applicable.

---

# Maintenance Rules

The changelog must:

* Remain chronological.
* Never delete historical releases.
* Preserve version history permanently.
* Clearly identify breaking changes.
* Keep the latest release near the top.
